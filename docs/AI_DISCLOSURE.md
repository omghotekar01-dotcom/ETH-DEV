**Transparency is required.** ChatGPT assisted ideation, sponsor research, UX composition, frontend templates, rules-engine logic, server and integration drafts, tests and documentation. Other tools or contributors must append truthful lines as work proceeds.

- `public/app.mjs`, `public/style.css`, `public/index.html`: AI-assisted UI design and implementation.
- `public/engine.mjs`: AI-assisted deterministic game logic. It is not an LLM.
- `server/`, `api/`, `scripts/`: AI-assisted drafts of optional real provider integrations. A working interface does not establish network verification.
- `tests/`, `docs/`, `arkiv/`: AI-assisted code/test/report drafts.
- Generated or reused visual assets: current interface uses code-generated CSS/SVG and standard Unicode symbols. No third-party proprietary art is included.

**Human contribution requirement:** Team members must actually implement or revise meaningful functionality, inspect and verify the code, conduct live sponsor integrations, present and explain their decisions, and document their own work. An entirely AI-created submission may be ineligible under ETHGlobal's rules. Do NOT falsify human contributions or present AI-only work as independent human engineering.

**Provenance:** Keep prompts/specs and incremental commits for hackathon review. Do not invent missing reviewer names or tests.
